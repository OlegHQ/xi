- Missing filter/fold providers, output floods, cancellation, malformed provider output, numeric `nrformats` variation and overflow are pinned by T104. Filter, fold, indent and format execution remains behind injected providers.
- A stale or missing child manifest is a qualification failure. At this checkpoint T102, T103 and T104 reports and manifests are present, their per-file checks pass, and the T105 manifest records the exact inputs.
