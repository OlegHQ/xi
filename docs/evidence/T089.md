## Failure cases and recovery

The owned fixtures reject overlapping selections/edits, stale document or selection generations, invalid endpoints, pointer capture loss and disposal after view closure. The scale artifact retains p50/p95/p99/max values for review, and the retention artifact records RSS, JS heap, external and ArrayBuffer counters before and after explicit Bun GC windows. In the latest 10,000-cycle window, 13.13 MiB of RSS growth was outside the JS heap and external counters, so the artifact identifies a native allocator/runtime component without misclassifying it as a proven application leak.

## Limitations and next action

T061's complete 597-row Vim inventory audit is a prerequisite and remains unfinished. The required MC01–MC12, MP01–MP04 and E19–E25 production PTY matrix and complete screenshot review are not all represented by these local probes. Keep T089 and downstream T062–T066 unproven until those acceptance items, the long-window retention issue and the complete journey matrix have evidence.
