## Performance and visual evidence

The retained captures are real terminal screenshots from XTerm 390, with truecolor/no-color and 120x40/80x24/60x18 variants. Visual line selection showed a continuous fill across the selected rows; Visual character and block captures showed the cursor and selected glyphs without clipping; empty-line and EOF captures retained an explicit cursor cell; the narrow no-color capture kept both cursor markers and the textual mode/count status legible. The frame artifact adds cell-level evidence for seven semantic endpoint and selection states, and the surface artifact drives every currently exported overlay renderable through its bounded state model. This is a focused E23/T034/T063 review, not the full E01–E17 and E19–E25 journey matrix.

## Failure cases and recovery

T087 covers stale presentation, reduced-motion, no-color, narrow viewport and renderer disposal. T034 covers tiny-terminal and resize cleanup. The surface matrix checks bounded output and explicit degraded messages, while its artifact records keyboard-only and assistive-technology behavior as unproven. No mandatory mouse or Nerd Font requirement is introduced by these surfaces.

## Limitations and next action

The complete visual UX gate still lacks named inspection of every relevant E01–E17 state, keyboard-only journey coverage, inactive-view captures, contrast measurements across every terminal palette/theme combination, and real assistive-technology testing. The frame and surface artifacts record the remaining unrepresented states and explicitly do not claim screen-reader or platform accessibility support. Keep T063 unproven until that matrix is retained and reviewed.
