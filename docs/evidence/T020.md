## Observable result

`resolveVimTextObject` produces source-versioned UTF-16 ranges for the built-in word, WORD, sentence, paragraph, quote, bracket, and tag objects. Its adapter feeds the central range/operator path. `extendVimVisualTextObject` expands the active selection while preserving its anchor and direction. The pinned d/c/y matrix verifies selected register payload/type, text edits, mode, and cursor placement; Visual snapshots verify selected text and active endpoint.

The operator planner also handles zero-width inner objects: delete is a no-edit/register-preserving operation, yank writes the empty characterwise value observed by Neovim, and change enters Insert at the gap without writing registers. The change plan exposes `registerEffect.noOp` so a consumer can preserve existing register state. T020 prepares effects only; the document remains the mutable text owner and commits remain a document-layer responsibility.

## Performance and visual evidence

T020 specifies no latency threshold, and the implementation does not render UI. Delimiter, sentence, and paragraph scans enforce the resolver's configurable scan bound (65,536 UTF-16 units by default, capped at 1,000,000). No performance or rendered-visual pass is claimed; broader keystroke latency remains a separate release gate.

## Failure cases and recovery

The required cursor-on-whitespace cases are `T020-ORACLE-DAW-WHITESPACE-01` and `T020-ORACLE-DIW-WHITESPACE-01`. The unclosed bracket and tag failures are `T020-ORACLE-DI-PAREN-UNCLOSED-01` and `T020-ORACLE-DIT-UNCLOSED-TAG-01`; they return `object-not-found` without a transaction or register effect. Empty quoted content is covered by `T020-ORACLE-DIQUOTE-EMPTY-01` and the seeded d/y/c fixtures `T020-ORACLE-DIQUOTE-EMPTY-REGISTER-01`, `T020-ORACLE-YIQUOTE-EMPTY-REGISTER-01`, and `T020-ORACLE-CIQUOTE-EMPTY-REGISTER-01`. These pin no-op deletion, empty yank register contents, Insert-gap placement, unchanged text where applicable, and preservation of preexisting registers for delete/change.

Nested delimiters/tags and escaped quote boundaries are covered by `T020-ORACLE-DI-PAREN-NESTED-01`, `T020-ORACLE-DA-BRACKET-01`, `T020-ORACLE-D2A-PAREN-COUNT-01`, `T020-ORACLE-DIT-NESTED-TAG-01`, `T020-ORACLE-DAT-NESTED-TAG-01`, `T020-ORACLE-D2AT-TAG-COUNT-01`, and `T020-ORACLE-DIQUOTE-ESCAPED-01`. Forward/backward selection expansion and anchor retention are covered by the `T020-ORACLE-VIW-EXPAND-*` fixtures and Visual paragraph/sentence/tag fixtures.

## Limitations and next action

The fixtures establish the enumerated Neovim 0.12.4 behaviors; they do not claim complete Vim compatibility for untested option matrices or malformed-language edge cases. Register effects are prepared, not committed; callers must honor the zero-width change effect's `noOp` flag. T061 owns reconciling the broader compatibility inventory. No T020-listed acceptance item remains untested; ledger status and final integration review remain with the parent agent.
