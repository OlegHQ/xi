## Observable result

The owned Vim session publishes immutable pending-key and parser-continuation metadata. The workbench schedules passive help using an independent timer and captured registry/config/focus generations. The OpenTUI panel is lazy, read-only and bounded; it renders actual production parser prefixes (`d`, `g`, Ctrl-W), literal `f`, and leader help without taking focus or dispatching keys. Completed and cancelled prefixes clear the model and timer.

## Performance and visual evidence

The help controller uses one bounded timeout per pending prefix and no render loop. The production PTY fixture observes the rendered text after the delay. Startup measurement remains diagnostic and is reported under T116; no T082 latency qualification is claimed.

## Failure cases and recovery

Config, registry and focus generation changes suppress stale delayed models. Completion, Escape and disposal clear callbacks. Missing capabilities remain visible with their typed reason. Literal continuations remain prompts and do not become filters.
